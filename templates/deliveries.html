Deliveries

Where every event stands on its way to Google Calendar, when the waiting ones are tried again, and what Almanac gave up on.

Delivery chain: where every event stands

An event passes four stages: received at the door, written to the journal, picked up by the worker and put in Google Calendar; the stage that is failing is lit red, and an event Almanac gave up on waits in the last lane.

Retry schedule: when the waiting entries are tried next

While Google refuses, Almanac tries all waiting entries together after 15 s, 1 min, 5 min, 15 min and then every 30 min; this shows the coming tries and, per entry, how many tries it has left.

An entry Almanac gave up on

After the third permanent refusal Almanac sets an event aside and tells you what is wrong and what to do about it.
